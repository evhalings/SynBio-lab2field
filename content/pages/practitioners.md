## How to connect with researchers

Academic synthetic biology labs are often genuinely looking for real-world problems to work on — but they need practitioners to help define the problem, not just receive the solution.

### A good first conversation includes

- A specific, concrete problem — not just "we need better technology"
- What infrastructure is actually available on the ground: power, refrigeration, trained staff, regulatory bodies
- Willingness to co-design from the start, with local staff or scientists as genuine partners — not just field-test hosts
- Realistic timelines — meaningful field-ready technology takes years, not months
