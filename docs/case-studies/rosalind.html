Case 1

ROSALIND, Cell-Free Water Biosensing

Lucks Lab, Northwestern · Field study: Kenya · 2023

A paper-based test that detects contaminants like fluoride in drinking water, with no lab, no cold storage, and a color readout non-experts can interpret.

98.2%
of 36 Kenyan participants read the test correctly themselves
89.5%
accuracy vs. gold-standard fluoride photometer, Kenya
<$10
cost per test
57
water samples tested, 36 households, Nakuru County
17
contaminants detectable by the platform, including lead (Chicago pilot ongoing)

The technology

  • Cell-free RNA biosensing platform
  • Freeze-dried, so no cold chain is required
  • Fluorescent visual readout that non-experts can interpret

What made it field-deployable

  • No cold chain required
  • Cell-free system: no GMO release
  • Social science from project inception
  • Usability was a core metric
  • Kenyan co-investigators embedded

What the partnership looked like

  • Kenyan co-investigators embedded from the start
  • Usability tested as a core design metric, not an afterthought
  • No cold chain and no GMO release, so an easier regulatory path
Technology was designed at Northwestern. A path to local production is a logical step toward full community independence.

"For impactful technology you need to understand the whole ecosystem — technical, regulatory, political."

Julius Lucks · Northwestern — ROSALIND
