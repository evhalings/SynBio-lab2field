Case 3 — Read with care

Gene Drive Mosquitoes

Imperial College London + Tanzanian partners · 2025

Mosquitoes engineered to resist malaria parasites, designed to spread the trait through wild populations.

Read with care

This is the highest-stakes technology in this guide — an ecosystem-level intervention, not a one-time test. The modification is designed to spread through wild populations, so it carries the highest regulatory and ethical stakes of any technology in this analysis.

Field release has not yet occurred for gene drive mosquitoes anywhere. This is presented as an example of the stakes of partnership design, not a ready-to-deploy solution.

The technology

  • Engineered to express antimicrobial peptides
  • Tested against the locally circulating parasite from Tanzanian children, not lab-cultured strains

What made it field-ready

  • Engineering done in Tanzania
  • Insectary built at Ifakara Health Institute, Bagamoyo
  • Local strains engineered against local P. falciparum isolates
  • Tanzanian scientists are co-creators
  • Community consent and public engagement conducted throughout

Why partnership matters as much as the science

Two programs show why the partnership model matters as much as the science.

Tanzania

Engineering done locally at the Ifakara Health Institute, against locally circulating parasite strains, with Tanzanian scientists as co-creators and ongoing community consent — seen as a promising model.

Burkina Faso

Program suspended in August 2025 amid civil society opposition and sovereignty concerns — the technology-first approach lacked the same local co-ownership.
